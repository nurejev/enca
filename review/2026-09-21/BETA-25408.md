# Beta 25408 — the preview scrolls on its own

Mihai, 21 Sep: "make preview windows scrollable separate from the main windows."

## What was wrong

25406 gave nine tools a shared list/detail presentation, but the two columns were
two columns of ONE growing page. Reading to the bottom of a long detail scrolled
the results list off the top, and picking the next row meant scrolling the whole
page back up. On 🕓 Changes with a full diff per event the page ran to thousands
of pixels and the list was unreachable from most of it. 25406 had also made
`#workspaceInspector` `position:static; max-height:none`, undoing the sticky
inspector `css/workspace.css` had given the policy workspace.

## What it does now

`css/list-detail.css`: `.ld-list`, `.ld-panel`, `#listView` and
`#workspaceInspector` are each their own scroll container, pinned at
`--ld-top` = `--sticky-tools + 12px`. `--sticky-tools` is header + tool tabs +
the screen's tab strip + its toolbar, MEASURED by `syncStickyTops` and
re-measured by its ResizeObserver, so a wrapped toolbar or a second row of tool
tabs moves the panes instead of sliding them under. `overscroll-behavior:contain`
so a flick past the end of a pane does not take the page with it. `100dvh`, not
`100vh`, for the phone address bar.

`js/list-detail.js`: the panel is scrolled back to its own top on a change of
SELECTION and not on a change of section — a tab switch inside one item keeps its
place, which is what makes the tabs worth having.

## The bug found in the first cut, and the fix

A sticky box cannot hold its offset past the bottom of its own box. With the
panes capped at viewport height the page still had scroll left after they
pinned — `main`'s 80px bottom padding plus the 47px footer — and that last
stretch dragged both panes UP under the toolbar. Measured on the policy
workspace at 1440×900: at maximum scroll the panes' tops were at 156px with the
toolbar's bottom at 253px, so 109px of the list and of the inspector sat behind
the toolbar. Exactly the bug this change set out to fix, in a new place.

Two halves to the fix, and they have to add up:

* `--ld-foot` — the footer's height, measured by `syncStickyTops` beside the
  rest, subtracted from the pane. Measured, because the footer wraps to two and
  three rows on a narrow window.
* `main { padding-bottom: 0 }` above 900px on a screen that ends in a pane —
  it does not need it, the pane already ends where the footer begins.

The sum is an invariant that does not depend on how tall the content above the
panes is: page scroll left after pinning = pane height + footer − viewport +
`--ld-top` = −18px. Always 18px short of fully pinning, never past it.

Verified at 1440×900 after the fix: toolbar bottom 253, both panes' tops 283 at
maximum page scroll — 30px clear of it, footer directly beneath them.

## Verified (Chromium, demo tenant, ?demo=1)

* 1440×900 and 1280×720 — 📄 Policies with the detail panel open and 🌐 Named
  locations: panes sticky, own `max-height` and `overflow-y:auto`, and at
  MAXIMUM page scroll every pane's top is at or below the toolbar's bottom.
* The inspector scrolled internally (368 of 936px) with `#listView`'s own scroll
  position unchanged — the two really are apart.
* 390×844 and 320×700 — one column, one scrollbar, no nested pane scroll, the
  bottom navigation rail intact (the `main` padding rule is gated to ≥901px).
* 204/204 offline tests, plain-text and toolbar-order checks clean, no page
  errors at any of the four sizes.
